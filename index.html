Our Story

Eva & Fab

SEPTEMBER 2024 — FOREVER
Chapter One

September 2024

It started in a group called Mabrook. Eva was one of the co-admins and Fab was simply another member. They weren't close. They didn't know that this ordinary beginning would become one of the most important stories of their lives.

The Beginning

Eva had her little circle — Jeevan, Anzu and Layla. Fab was closer to Sangu. Everyone had their own people and their own world. And somehow, two people who weren't supposed to become close slowly started finding their way toward each other.

November 2024

A Choice

Eva's friends told her to keep her distance from Fab and Mabru. But Eva didn't listen. Little by little, things changed. She fought with Anzu, Layla and Jeevan... and somehow became closer to Fab.

January 2025

4 AM Conversations

They became close friends. They talked in Sharchat, sometimes until four in the morning. Eva even tried to bring Fab deeper into the group. What began as friendship was quietly becoming something neither of them could ignore.

The Question
"Do you love me?"

Fab asked Eva the question. She answered yes. He didn't say everything directly... but sometimes actions speak louder than words.

Closer

Their feelings slowly became obvious. Their conversations changed. Their interactions became noticeable even inside the group. The admins knew. The people around them knew. And somewhere between friendship and love, their story had already begun.

Instagram

A New Place

Then came a small fight. They deleted their Sharchat conversation. Instead of ending everything, they started talking on Instagram through their second accounts.

No Tomorrow

On that first day, they talked as if tomorrow didn't exist. Messages became longer. Night calls began. They were becoming more attached with every conversation.

September 2025

Silence

A video call became the reason for another fight. Eva refused. Things broke apart. Then came silence. September passed without messages, calls or contact.

October 2025

The Return

October 2 — Fab messaged Eva. She saw it but didn't reply. October 3 — another message. Then WhatsApp. Finally, on October 5, Eva replied.

And the story came back to life. ❤️
November 16, 2025

5:45 AM

At 11:30 PM they started a call. They talked until 5:45 AM. For those hours, they weren't just friends anymore. They talked like a real couple. The distance between them felt smaller.

Saudi Arabia → India

A Decision

Fab was working in a pharmacy in Saudi Arabia. Eva was working as a software developer. And then Fab decided to come to India. Suddenly, the person on the other side of the phone was going to be standing in front of her.

December 6, 2025

A Birthday Arrival

The flight was delayed. Fab finally landed in India on December 6 — Eva's birthday. But after arriving, life became busy with family, friends and election work. Calls became occasional. Eva missed him.

December 15, 2025

The First Meeting

Then came the day they had imagined so many times. December 15. They finally met. Eva was incredibly happy. They walked and walked — probably much more than Fab expected.

A Day Together

They went to Lulu Mall and watched Kalamkaval. Everything felt different when they were finally together. A day that had existed only in imagination had become real.

A Private Memory
❤️

They shared a private moment together, remembered not for the details, but for the closeness, trust and emotions they felt that day.

That Little Girl

Later that night, they went out to eat. They met a little girl who was about a year old. She liked Fab and giggled at him. Eva loved watching the moment. Sometimes the smallest memories become the ones we remember most.

5:30 AM

Watching You Sleep

The next morning, Eva woke around 5:30. Fab was sleeping deeply. She looked at his sleeping face and quietly loved the moment. Then he woke, kissed her, and they simply stayed close for a little longer.

Trivandrum Zoo

One More Place

They decided to go to the zoo because staying in the room would make it impossible to stop missing each other. So they went out. Around 2 PM, the day together finally came to an end.

After That Day

After they separated, the calls continued. Almost every day. The distance was back, but the connection was stronger than before.

A Difficult Truth

Too Late

Fab wanted to marry someone else. He asked Eva if it was okay. She said yes. He found someone and became engaged. Eva tried to create distance. But that was when she realized just how deeply she loved him.

The Realization

Eva finally asked if he could marry her. But by then, it was too late. He refused. Their conversations became dry. One message every couple of days. No calls. Just distance.

Two or Three Weeks Later

A Call

Then they called again. Both were sad. Both needed comfort. They consoled each other. Two months passed in that strange space between letting go and not being able to let go.

May 25, 2026

Calicut

Separation had made their connection stronger. Eva travelled to Calicut to meet Fab. She had decided there would be no touching, because she knew how easily missing each other could turn into wanting to stay close.

But Hearts Don't Follow Rules

They missed each other too much. The plans they made for distance didn't survive the emotions of finally being together again. They shared another private memory, held together by everything they had been through.

Forum Mall

Another Movie

They went to Forum Mall in Calicut and watched Drishyam 3. Another ordinary activity became another extraordinary memory simply because they were together.

Calicut Bus Stop

Don't Go Yet

Fab dropped Eva at the bus stop. Her bus was late. Instead of leaving, Fab stayed. They sat together for a long time. Sometimes love is simply choosing to stay a little longer.

The Words
"I truly love you."

Fab said it. Eva felt those words came from his heart. She wanted to kiss him, but the moment didn't allow it. Still, she carried those words with her.

After Calicut

Closer Again

They tried to separate themselves. Somehow, the opposite happened. Video calls returned. Calls became daily. The distance remained, but the connection refused to disappear.

September 9, 2026

Today

Eva is happy. She wants to meet Fab again. She doesn't know exactly when it will happen. But she knows one thing: if they get another full day together, she wants to make every minute count.
