Before We Became Us

💻

Eva's Little World

While our story was slowly beginning, Eva was busy building her own future. She was becoming a software developer, learning new things, working hard, dreaming about a better life and trying to become stronger every day. She had her own goals, her own responsibilities and her own little world. She didn't know that somewhere inside that busy life, another person was quietly becoming important.

💊

Fab's World

Far away from Eva, Fab was building his own life too. Working in pharmacy in Saudi Arabia, living away from home, carrying responsibilities, making plans and trying to create his own future. Two people. Two careers. Two different countries. And yet, somehow, their lives kept moving toward the same story.

Chapter by chapter

How We Found Each Other

Not every love story begins with love. Some begin with friendship, arguments, silly conversations and two people who don't realize what they are slowly becoming.

September 2024

Two Strangers in the Same Room

It all started inside a group called Sharchat. Eva was one of the co-admins. Fab was simply another member.

Eva had her own little circle — Jeevan, Anzu and Layla. Fab was closer to Sangu. We were surrounded by the same people, reading the same messages, laughing at the same things... but we were still strangers.

Neither of us knew that the person who looked like just another member would eventually become someone our heart would refuse to forget.

Sometimes the beginning of a love story looks completely ordinary.
🌱❤️
November 2024

The Person I Was Told to Avoid

Slowly, something changed. Eva's friends told her to keep some distance from Fab and Mabru.

But Eva didn't listen. Maybe she couldn't explain why. Maybe she simply felt comfortable around him. And after fights with Anzu, Layla and Jeevan, she somehow found herself becoming closer to Fab.

Isn't it funny? Sometimes everyone around you can see a person as a bad idea... while your heart quietly says, "But I want to know him."

And without knowing it, Eva was already choosing Fab.
🥹💕
January 2025

Four in the Morning

By January, we weren't strangers anymore. We had become close friends.

Our conversations sometimes continued until four in the morning. Everyone else was sleeping, but somehow we always had one more thing to say.

Eva even tried to bring Fab more into the group. Fab still had his attachment to Sangu, but something between Eva and Fab was becoming harder to ignore.

Then came the question:

"Do you love me?"

Eva said yes. Fab didn't clearly say those words back. But sometimes, love doesn't arrive as a perfect sentence. Sometimes it arrives through attention, late-night calls, small efforts and the way someone keeps coming back.

Maybe that was the first time our hearts knew something our mouths didn't.
🌙❤️
Later in 2025

When Everything Went Quiet

Then came a fight. The Sharchat conversation disappeared. And somehow, our little world moved to Instagram.

We started talking from our second IDs. Messages became calls. Calls became something we waited for.

Fab asked for a video call. Eva refused. Another fight happened. And then... silence.

No messages. No calls. No late-night conversations. Something that had slowly become part of everyday life suddenly wasn't there anymore.

Sometimes losing someone doesn't happen in one moment. Sometimes it happens one unanswered message at a time.
💔🌧️
September 2025

A Month Without Us

September passed. No calls. No messages. No "How was your day?"

Life continued. Work continued. Everything looked normal from the outside. But there are some people you can stop talking to without ever really stopping thinking about them.

Silence can be loud when the person you miss used to be part of your every day.
🥀
October 2–5, 2025

The Message That Came Back

On October 2, Fab sent a message on Instagram. Eva didn't reply.

The next day, another message came. Then WhatsApp too.

And finally... October 5. Eva replied.

To someone else, it might have looked like just a reply. But to us, it was a tiny door opening after a month of silence.

Sometimes one small "hi" is enough to restart an entire chapter.
📩❤️
November 16, 2025

11:30 PM — 5:45 AM

One call lasted from 11:30 at night until 5:45 in the morning.

Six hours. A whole night. And somehow, it still didn't feel long enough.

We talked like a couple. We became closer. The distance between our hearts suddenly felt smaller.

And somewhere during those conversations, Fab decided he wanted to come to India from Saudi Arabia.

After everything, the person who once disappeared was now travelling thousands of kilometres to come closer.
🌙📞❤️
The distance was finally getting smaller

The Journey Home ✈️

Saudi Arabia
India

December arrived. Fab was leaving Saudi Arabia and travelling all the way to India.

Eva was waiting. Perhaps she had imagined this moment a hundred different ways. Perhaps she was nervous. Perhaps she was smiling at her phone more than she wanted to admit.

🎂 Eva's birthday was December 5.

✈️ Fab finally landed in India on December 6 after the flight was delayed.

He didn't arrive exactly on her birthday. But maybe that didn't matter. Because the biggest gift wasn't something wrapped in paper. It was the fact that after all those months, all those kilometres, all those missed calls and all those nights of missing each other... he was finally here.

"Sometimes love doesn't arrive exactly when you expect it. It arrives a little late... but still right on time." ❤️
✈️☁️🎂❤️
December 15, 2025

The Day Screens Were No Longer Enough

And then... December 15.

The day we had been waiting for.

No screen. No phone speaker. No typing. No "good night" message from far away.

Finally... we were in the same place.

We walked together. We went to Lulu Mall. We watched Kalamkaval. We talked. We laughed. We simply enjoyed being beside each other.

After so much distance, even walking beside you felt special. Because for so long, I had only been able to imagine what it would feel like.

And suddenly, the person I missed through a screen was walking beside me.
🥹🤍
The Next Morning

The Quietest Memory

Around 5:30 in the morning, Eva watched Fab sleeping.

There was no special music. No beautiful location. No perfect movie scene. Just a quiet morning and the person she had spent so long missing.

Sometimes love isn't about grand gestures. Sometimes it's simply looking at someone while they sleep and thinking, "I can't believe you're actually here."

The smallest moments often become the memories we protect the most.
🌅🤍
That Day

Trying Not to Say Goodbye

We didn't want to spend the whole day indoors. So we went to Trivandrum Zoo.

We walked around. We talked. We tried to make every hour count.

But eventually, the clock moved forward. Around 2 PM, it was time to separate.

We knew goodbye was coming. But knowing doesn't make goodbye easier.

The hardest part of a beautiful day is knowing that eventually it has to end.
🦋💔
The Hardest Chapter

When Love Became Painful

After the meeting, we continued talking every day.

But then Fab told Eva that he wanted to marry someone else. He asked if she was okay with it. She tried to accept it.

Then he found someone. And the engagement happened.

Eva started distancing herself. She became sad. She cried. And somewhere inside all that pain, she finally understood something: she had fallen much deeper than she realized.

She asked if he could marry her instead. But by then, it was already too late.

Sometimes we understand the value of someone exactly when life starts teaching us how to live without them.
💔🥺
The Following Months

Somewhere Between Letting Go & Holding On

Messages became dry. Sometimes only one message every couple of days.

For two or three weeks, everything felt distant.

Then the calls returned. Conversations returned. Comfort returned.

For almost two months, we lived somewhere between letting go and holding on.

We were trying to teach our hearts how to stay away from each other... while our hearts were still choosing each other.
🌧️❤️
May 25, 2026

One More Time

Eva travelled to Calicut to meet Fab.

This time she had already made a plan:

"No touch. Just meet and go."

But hearts don't always follow plans.

We missed each other too much. We spent time together. We went to Forum Mall. We watched Drishyam 3. We laughed. We created another memory we never planned to create.

Then came the bus stop.

Fab dropped Eva at the Calicut bus stop. The bus was late. And he stayed.

He could have left. But he waited.

Sometimes love isn't a huge speech. Sometimes it's simply: "I'll wait until your bus comes."

And then Fab told Eva that he truly loved her.

This time, she didn't just hear the words. She felt them.

Maybe that bus stop wasn't just a place. Maybe it became another little piece of our story that we will never forget.
🚌❤️🥹
Now — September 2026

Somehow, We're Still Here

After that meeting, we tried again to separate.

But then the calls became daily. Video calls became normal. Hearing each other's voice became part of everyday life again.

Maybe we still don't know exactly where this story is going.

Maybe there are difficult chapters ahead. Maybe there are things neither of us can predict.

But right now... Eva is happy.

She wants to meet Fab again. She doesn't know when. She doesn't know how.

She only knows that if they ever get one full day together again, she wants to fill it with everything.

Talking. Walking. Laughing. Eating. Watching something together. Taking silly pictures. Making stupid jokes. And simply being close.

Because after everything we've been through, even an ordinary day together feels extraordinary.
♾️❤️

With love,
