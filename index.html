Chapter one

How it all started...

💬
September 2024

A group chat...

It started with something so ordinary — a group called Mabrook on Sharchat. Eva was one of the admins and Fab was just another member.

They weren't particularly close. They didn't know that this little group chat was quietly preparing the beginning of their story.

👀 Nobody knew what was coming...
🥹
November 2024

“Stay away from him.”

Eva's friends told her to distance herself from Fab and Mabru.

But Eva didn't listen. Instead, after a fight with Anzu, Layla and Jeevan, she somehow became even closer to Fab.

🙈 She probably should have listened...
🌙
January 2025

4 AM conversations

Somewhere between random conversations, group chats and staying awake until 4 AM, something changed.

Eva started making an effort to keep Fab around. Fab was still attached to Sangu, but Eva and Fab were becoming closer.

🌙 4 AM was apparently their time.
❤️
The question

“Do you love me?”

Then came the question.

Fab asked Eva, “Do you love me?”

And Eva said yes.

He didn't clearly say what he felt, but his actions were starting to say enough.

🥰 The group definitely noticed.
💔
The first goodbye

A little break...

A fight happened. The Sharchat conversation was deleted. Then came Instagram's second accounts, late-night conversations and calls.

There was even a video-call request... but Eva refused.

Another fight followed. And eventually, they stopped talking.

🥺 But that wasn't really the end.
📱
September 2025

Silence

September passed without contact.

Sometimes people leave your life without realizing they are leaving a space that nobody else can fill.

💌
October 2025

“Hey...”

October 2nd. Fab messaged Eva on Instagram.

No reply.

October 3rd. Another message. Then WhatsApp.

October 5th... Eva finally replied.

🥹 And somehow... they found each other again.
🌙❤️
November 16, 2025

11:30 PM → 5:45 AM

One call.

Six hours of talking. Like nothing had changed. Like the distance had never happened.

Except this time... they were even more attached.

🌙 Sleep was cancelled.
✈️
December 2025

He came to India

Fab worked in a pharmacy in Saudi Arabia. Eva was building her career as a software developer.

And then Fab decided to come to India.

His flight was delayed... and he finally landed on December 6 — Eva's birthday.

🎂 What a birthday surprise.
🫶
December 15, 2025

The first meeting

December 15.

This time there were no screens, no typing bubbles, and no “good night” messages.

They were finally standing next to each other.

Walking. Lulu Mall. Kalamkaval. Laughing. A private moment together. Food. And memories that neither of them could simply delete.

🥰 Finally... real life.
❤️

Some moments don't need photographs.

Sometimes your heart remembers everything — the place, the smile, the silence, the way someone looked at you, and how it felt when they were finally there.

🌅
The next morning

5:30 AM

Eva watched Fab sleeping.

Then he woke up. A kiss. Another unforgettable morning. And eventually, sleep again.

🥹 Some mornings stay forever.
🦁
Later that day

Trivandrum Zoo

They decided they shouldn't spend the entire day inside.

So they went to Trivandrum Zoo and eventually separated around 2 PM.

🐒 At least the animals got to see them.
💔
The difficult part

“I'm going to marry someone else.”

Fab told Eva that he wanted to marry someone else.

Then he found the girl. Then came the engagement.

Eva tried to distance herself. She became sad. She cried. And somewhere in all that pain, she finally understood how deeply she loved him.

📞
The next two months

Not together... not apart

Messages became dry. Every couple of days. No calls.

Then one call would happen. One person would console the other. And the cycle would begin again.

🥺 Hearts don't always know when to leave.
🚌❤️
May 25, 2026

Calicut

Eva travelled to Calicut to meet Fab.

At first she decided: “No touching.”

But sometimes you can make rules with your mind... while your heart has already decided.

They spent time together, went to Forum Mall, watched Drishyam 3, and eventually Fab dropped Eva at the Calicut bus stop.

Her bus was late. He stayed and waited.

🥹 He didn't leave until she was okay.
❤️
That day

“I truly love you.”

Fab told Eva that he truly loved her.

And this time, she didn't just hear the words.

She felt them.

📱💕
After Calicut

Trying to say goodbye

They tried to separate again.

But the calls became daily. Video calls. Conversations. Little moments.

Apparently, goodbye wasn't working very well.

😂❤️ Failed successfully.
