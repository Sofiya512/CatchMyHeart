THE NEXT MORNING

5:30 AM

THE PAINFUL CHAPTER

A QUESTION

"Can you marry me?"

Eva finally asked the question that had been living inside her heart.

But by then... it was too late.

Fab refused.

They tried to live with the distance.

THE DISTANCE

📱

Messages came every few days. No more regular calls. Sometimes weeks passed.

Then one day... they called.

Both were sad. Both consoled each other.

And somehow... the separation made their connection even stronger.

MAY 25, 2026

🚍❤️

A Letter From Eva ❤️

Fab,

If someone had told me in September 2024 that a person from a random group would become such an important part of my life, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

We started as two people who weren't even that close.

Then came the late-night conversations, the silly fights, the calls, the silence, the distance, and all the moments that somehow brought us back to each other.

I don't know what you felt during every moment of our story, but I know what I felt.

I was happy when I was with you. I missed you when you were away. And I realized how much you meant to me when I thought I had lost you.

I still remember December 15. Walking beside you. Watching a movie. Spending those little hours together. And waking up and seeing you sleeping.

I remember Calicut too. Sitting at the bus stop and hearing you tell me that you truly loved me.

Those moments are mine forever, no matter what happens next.

I don't know where our story will end. Maybe neither of us knows.

But if life gives us another day together, I want to fill it with everything we missed.

More walking. More talking. More laughing. More memories.

And maybe... just maybe... another chapter.

Whatever happens, thank you for becoming one of the most unforgettable parts of my life.

I love you. ❤️

— Eva ❤️
