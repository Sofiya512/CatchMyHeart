Our little timeline

How We Became Us

September 2024

Before We Knew

It started inside a group called Sharchat. Eva was one of the co-admins and Fab was simply another member of the group.

There were other people around us. Eva had her own little circle with Jeevan, Anzu and Layla, while Fab was closer to Sangu. We were there at the same time, talking to the same people, laughing in the same group... but somehow we were still strangers.

Neither of us knew that the person who seemed like just another member would eventually become one of the most important people in the other's life.

🌱❤️
November 2024

The First Change

Things slowly started changing. Eva's friends told her to keep some distance from Fab and Mabru.

But Eva didn't listen. Somewhere inside, she didn't want to push him away. And after fights with Anzu, Layla and Jeevan, she somehow found herself becoming closer to Fab.

Maybe love doesn't always begin with a grand moment. Sometimes it begins when you choose someone's company even when everyone else tells you not to.

🥹💕
January 2025

4 AM Conversations

By January, we were no longer just people from the same group. We had become close friends.

Sometimes our conversations continued until four in the morning. The kind of conversations where you forget the time because talking to one person feels more interesting than sleeping.

Eva even made efforts to bring Fab into the group more. Fab still had his attachment to Sangu, but something between Eva and Fab was quietly growing.

Then came the question:

"Do you love me?"

Eva said yes. Fab didn't clearly say the words back, but sometimes actions speak much louder than words.

🌙❤️
Later in 2025

When Everything Went Quiet

Then came a fight. The Sharchat conversation disappeared, and somehow our little world moved to Instagram.

We started talking from our second IDs. The first day felt intense. Messages turned into calls, and calls slowly became something we waited for.

Fab asked for a video call. Eva refused. Another fight happened. Then came silence.

We stopped talking. What had become a daily part of life suddenly became nothing.

Sometimes losing someone doesn't happen in one dramatic moment. Sometimes it happens through a few unanswered messages and longer nights.

💔🌧️
September 2025

A Month Without Us

September passed without contact. No calls. No late-night conversations. No little messages to ask how the day went.

Life continued for both of us, but there are some people you can stop talking to without ever really stopping thinking about them.

🥀
October 2–5, 2025

The Message That Came Back

On October 2, Fab sent a message on Instagram. Eva didn't reply.

The next day another message came. Then WhatsApp too.

And finally, on October 5, Eva replied.

Maybe that tiny reply looked ordinary to everyone else. But for us, it opened a door that we thought had already been closed.

Somehow, after everything, we found our way back to each other.

📩❤️
November 16, 2025

11:30 PM to 5:45 AM

One call lasted from 11:30 at night until 5:45 in the morning.

Six hours. A whole night. And somehow it still didn't feel enough.

We talked like a couple. We became closer than before. The distance between us suddenly felt smaller.

Somewhere during those conversations, Fab decided he wanted to come to India from Saudi Arabia.

Two people who had once stopped talking were now planning how to finally see each other.

🌙📞❤️
December 2025

The Journey Home

Fab worked in Saudi Arabia, while Eva was building her life as a software developer.

Then December came. Fab travelled all the way to India.

The flight was delayed, and after all the waiting, he finally landed on December 6.

Eva's birthday was actually the day before — December 5.

It wasn't the birthday meeting she might have imagined, but somehow the most important gift of that time was knowing that the person she missed was finally close to home.

✈️🎂❤️
December 15, 2025

The Day We Finally Met

And then came the day we had been waiting for.

December 15.

Not a screen. Not a voice through a phone. Not a message. Finally... us, in the same place.

We walked together. We spent time at Lulu Mall. We watched Kalamkaval. We talked, laughed and simply enjoyed being beside each other.

After all the months of missing, there was something incredibly comforting about finally being able to look at each other instead of looking at a screen.

That day gave us memories that neither distance nor time could easily take away.

🥹🤍
The Next Morning

A Quiet Morning

Around 5:30 in the morning, Eva watched Fab sleeping.

There is something special about watching someone you love when they don't know you are looking at them.

No messages. No calls. No distance. Just one quiet room and the person you had spent so long missing.

When Fab woke up, the morning became another private memory that belonged only to us.

Sometimes the smallest moments become the ones we remember the longest.

🌅🤍
That Day

Trying Not To Say Goodbye

We didn't want to spend the entire day indoors, so we went to Trivandrum Zoo.

We walked around, spent time together, and tried to make every hour count.

But eventually it was time to separate. Around 2 PM, we went our different ways.

The strange thing about goodbye is that you can know it is coming and still not be ready for it.

🦋💔
The Hardest Chapter

When Love Became Painful

After the meeting, we continued talking every day. But then Fab told Eva that he wanted to marry someone else.

He asked if she was okay with it. She tried to accept it.

Then he found someone. And the engagement happened.

Eva started distancing herself. She was sad. She cried. And somewhere in all that pain, she finally understood just how deeply she had fallen in love.

She asked if he could marry her instead. But by then, it was too late.

Sometimes you understand the value of a person exactly when life starts teaching you how to live without them.

💔🥺
The Following Months

Still There

Messages became dry. Sometimes only one message every couple of days. It continued for two or three weeks.

Then there were calls again. Conversations. Comfort. Two people trying to understand how to stay away from something that still felt so close.

For almost two months, we lived somewhere between letting go and holding on.

🌧️❤️
May 25, 2026

One More Meeting

Eva travelled to Calicut to meet Fab.

This time she had decided:
"No touch. Just meet and go."

But hearts don't always follow plans.

We missed each other too much. We spent time together, went to Forum Mall, watched Drishyam 3, and created another chapter we never planned to write.

When it was finally time to leave, Fab dropped Eva at the Calicut bus stop.

The bus was late. And he stayed there waiting.

That small act meant more than a thousand words. He told her that he truly loved her.

And this time, Eva didn't just hear it. She felt it.

🚌❤️🥹
Now — September 2026

And Somehow, We're Still Here

After that meeting, we tried again to separate.

But the calls became daily. Video calls became normal. Hearing each other's voice became part of everyday life again.

Maybe we don't know exactly where this story is going.

Maybe there are still difficult chapters ahead.

But right now, Eva is happy.

She wants to meet Fab again. She doesn't know when. She doesn't know how.

She only knows that if they ever get one full day together again, she wants to fill it with everything: talking, walking, laughing, eating, watching something together, taking silly pictures, and simply being close.

Because after everything we've been through, even an ordinary day together feels like something extraordinary.

♾️❤️

A Little Letter For You 💌

If you have reached this far, then you have walked through almost every chapter of our story.

You have seen the beginning, the late-night conversations, the silence, the return, the waiting, the meetings, the tears, and all the little moments that made us who we are.

I don't know what the future has written for us. But I know that whatever happens, I will always be grateful that our paths crossed.

You became a memory before I even realized you were becoming one. And somehow, even after everything, a part of me still chooses you.

So if there is one thing I want you to remember, it is this:

"You were never just a chapter in my life. You became part of the story itself."

With love,
Eva ❤️
