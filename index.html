Our timeline

The chapters we lived

September 2024

Two strangers in the same room

There was a group called Mabrook. Eva was one of the admins and Fab was simply another member. We weren't particularly close. We didn't know that the person sitting quietly in the background would eventually become such a huge part of the story.

November 2024

The person I was told to avoid

People told me to stay away from you. Somehow, I did the exact opposite. I fought with people I cared about, became closer to you, and slowly started choosing your presence without even realizing what my heart was doing.

January 2025

Four in the morning

We became close. Conversations stretched past midnight, then past 2 AM, then somehow reached 4 AM. There was always something more to say. Somewhere in those sleepless nights, friendship started feeling like something else.

Later in 2025

When everything went quiet

Then came the fight. Conversations disappeared. The group was gone. We moved to Instagram and somehow even that couldn't save us from another fight. Then suddenly... silence.

September 2025

A month without us

A whole month passed without talking. Sometimes silence is strange. You don't realize how much space someone occupies in your life until that space suddenly becomes empty.

October 2 — 5, 2025

The message that came back

October 2. A message appeared. I didn't answer. October 3, another message. Then WhatsApp. Finally, October 5... I replied. And somehow, a story that looked finished quietly opened another chapter.

November 16, 2025

11:30 PM — 5:45 AM

Six hours. One night. So many conversations. We talked like two people who had finally stopped pretending they didn't care. That night, something changed. We became closer than before.

December 15, 2025

The day screens were no longer enough

After so many messages, calls and late-night conversations, we finally stood in front of each other. We walked. We went to Lulu Mall. We watched Kalamkaval. We talked. We laughed. And for a little while, the rest of the world didn't matter.

🌙

The quietest memory

The next morning, while everything around us was quiet, there was a moment that felt like it belonged only to us. Sometimes the smallest moments become the ones the heart remembers longest.

After the meeting

Trying not to say goodbye

We went to the zoo. We spent time together. And around 2 PM, we had to separate again. It was strange how quickly one beautiful day could become another memory.

The difficult chapter

When love became painful

Then came the words I never wanted to hear. You told me you wanted to marry someone else. I tried to act like I was okay. I wasn't. When you found someone and got engaged, I finally understood how deeply I had fallen for you.

The in-between

Somewhere between letting go & holding on

For weeks, our messages became dry. Then the calls returned. We comforted each other. We tried to move on. We tried to hold on. For almost two months, our hearts lived somewhere between goodbye and maybe.

🚌
May 25, 2026 • Calicut

One more time

I travelled to Calicut with one simple decision: no touch, just meet and go. But hearts don't always follow plans. We missed each other too much. We went to Forum Mall. We watched Drishyam 3. We laughed. We made another memory. And when you dropped me at the bus stop, you waited because my bus was late.

Then you told me you truly loved me. And somehow, those words stayed with me.

September 2026

Somehow, we're still here.

We tried to separate again. But calls became daily. Video calls became normal. And somewhere along the way, we stopped trying so hard to erase each other from our lives.
